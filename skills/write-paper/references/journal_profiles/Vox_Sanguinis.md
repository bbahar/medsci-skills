## Manuscript Types and Word Limits

Word limits are **totals**: they include the title page, abstract, main text, references, figure legends and tables, and exclude only author names and affiliations. This is stricter than journals that count body text alone — count everything.

| Type | Total Word Limit | Abstract | References | Figures/Tables |
|------|------------------|----------|------------|----------------|
| Original Article | 5,000 | 250 (structured) | Not stated | Not stated |
| Review | 6,600 | 250 (scope summary; narrative reviews need not use the structured headings) | Not stated | Not stated |
| Short Report | 2,500 | 200 | 12 max | 3 max (figures and/or tables combined) |
| Commentary | 1,500 including references | None | Counted in the 1,500 | 1 figure + 1 table max |
| Letter to the Editor | 800 | None | 10 max | Not stated |
| Report (ISBT Working Party meetings) | ~6,600 | 200 (need not be structured) | Not stated | Not stated |
| Obituary | 600 (excluding author names and affiliations) | None | Not stated | Headshot of the person |
| International Forum | Topic approved by the section editor | — | — | — |

- **Commentary** and **Report**: contact the Editor-in-Chief before submitting to confirm the topic is of interest.
- **International Forum**: contact the section editor responsible for the International Forum.
- **Case Reports**: submission is not encouraged.
- Original Articles are judged on novelty and importance to the field.

## Abstract Requirements

Required for Original Articles and Reviews; maximum 250 words, text only, no abbreviations and no citations.

```
Background and Objectives: What is the purpose of the study?
Materials and Methods: How was the study done?
Results: Most important findings?
Conclusion: Most important conclusion?
```

Review abstracts define the subject area and summarize the scope; narrative reviews need not follow the structure above. Short Reports and Reports carry a 200-word abstract (Reports need not be structured).

Also required with the abstract:
- **Keywords**: three to six, chosen from the options provided at submission.
- **Highlights**: three bullet points on the key findings and implications (Original Articles, Reviews and Reports).

## Required Sections (Original Article)

Main text file order (tables inside the main file; figures and supporting information as separate files):

1. **Title page** — informative title with the major keywords and **no abbreviations**; running title under 40 characters; full author names and affiliations; corresponding author(s) (maximum two) with addresses and emails; conflict-of-interest statement for all authors (or "Conflict of Interest: The authors have no conflict of interest"); funding (or "Funding: The author(s) received no specific funding for this work"); **Data Availability Statement**; **total word count**.
2. **Acknowledgements** — each author's specific contribution by initials (e.g. "A.B. performed the research and wrote the first draft…"); non-author contributors with their permission; named professional medical writers and who paid them; any AI-tool use (see AI policy). Thanks to anonymous reviewers are not appropriate.
3. **Abstract and keywords**
4. **Highlights** (three bullets)
5. **Main text** — Introduction; a single **Materials and Methods** section covering the methodology of all experiments; Results; Discussion. Footnotes in the text are not allowed — incorporate as parenthetical matter.
6. **References**
7. **Tables** (each with title and footnotes)
8. **Figure legends**
9. **Appendices** (if relevant; supplied as separate files, referred to in the text)

Formatting: Arial 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing. British spelling is applied in production (either spelling accepted at submission). SI units. Numbers under 10 spelt out except with units, ages, or in lists with other numbers. Generic drug and chemical names only; proprietary products by generic name with the proprietary name and manufacturer in parentheses.

Methods must name the approving ethics committee, confirm conformity with recognised standards (e.g. Declaration of Helsinki), and state that informed consent was obtained. Animal studies name the approving body and the guidelines followed.

**References**: numbered in order of first citation, cited as Arabic numerals in square brackets; list up to six authors, then the first six followed by ", et al."; Index Medicus journal abbreviations; no punctuation other than commas between authors. Unpublished material is cited as "unpublished data" in the text, not in the list. Incorrectly formatted references can get the paper returned at submission — use the Vox Sanguinis style file (EndNote / Zotero CSL `vox-sanguinis`).

```
Kim CH, Simmons SC, Wang D, Najafzadeh P, Azad A, Pham HP. An economic analysis of different treatments for bleeding in patients with acquired haemophilia. Vox Sang. 2020;115:192-9.
```

## Statistical Reporting

The guidelines specify only table conventions for statistics; the remaining items are defaults to verify:

- Statistical measures such as SD or SEM are identified **in the table headings** (stated).
- Table footnotes use superscript letters a, b, c, kept to a minimum and in bold; all abbreviations defined in footnotes (stated).
- Report exact p-values to 2–3 significant figures, p < 0.001 below that threshold [VERIFY — default, not stated].
- 95% CI for primary outcomes [VERIFY — default, not stated].
- Effect sizes in clinically meaningful units [VERIFY — default, not stated].
- Name the statistical software and version [VERIFY — default, not stated].

## Figures

- **Maximum**: Not stated for Original Articles; Short Reports ≤ 3 figures/tables combined; Commentary ≤ 1 figure + 1 table.
- **Resolution**: A wide range of formats, sizes and resolutions is accepted for peer review; post-acceptance requirements follow Wiley's electronic artwork guidelines.
- **Format**: Separate files from the main text.
- **Color**: Submit all figures, including graphs and charts, in colour; colour reproduction is free of charge.
- Tables must be editable (not images, not made in graphics software), self-contained, and not duplicate the text.

## Common Rejection Reasons

Derived from the journal's own author guidelines (not from editor-decision data):

1. Judged not competitive, or outside the interests of most of the readership — returned without full review at the Editor-in-Chief's discretion.
2. Insufficient novelty or importance to the field — the explicit criterion for Original Articles; papers are judged against other submissions under consideration.
3. Over the word limit — the count includes references, tables and legends, so a body-text-only count overshoots.
4. References not in the journal's style — the guidelines warn the paper may be returned after submission.
5. Case reports — submission is not encouraged; consider a Short Report only if the findings are new, even if initial or limited.

## AI Writing Disclosure Policy

- **Requirement level:** Required when an AI-generated-content (AIGC) tool was used to develop any portion of the manuscript.
- **Permitted scope:** Use is allowed if disclosed; AIGC tools cannot be authors (they cannot initiate research, be accountable, or hold copyright — per COPE's position statement). Tools used only to improve spelling, grammar and general editing are outside the policy. The editor makes the final decision on whether a given use is appropriate.
- **Disclosure location:** Methods **or** Acknowledgements, described transparently and in detail. The author is fully responsible for the accuracy of tool-provided information and for referencing the work it depends on.
- **AI-generated images:** Not specified in the author guidelines.
- **Policy URL:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14230410/homepage/forauthors.html (no dedicated AI policy page)

## Positioning

**Choose Vox Sanguinis when**: the work is a novel development in transfusion medicine or cellular therapies with international relevance — blood components and donors, transfusion practice and safety, immunohaematology, or work aligned with ISBT Working Parties. The single-blind review and the 5,000-word total cap suit focused original studies; a Short Report suits initial or limited findings.

**Avoid Vox Sanguinis for**: single case reports (discouraged); long papers whose references and tables push the total past 5,000 words; work whose main interest is outside transfusion medicine and cellular therapy.
