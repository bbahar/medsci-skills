## Article Types Accepted
- Regular Article (4,000 words Introduction–Discussion; 250-word single-paragraph abstract; 7 figures; ~100 references; Key Points + visual abstract; systematic reviews/meta-analyses go here)
- Brief Report (1,500 words; 200-word abstract; 3 figures/tables; 25 references; Key Points + visual abstract)
- Letter to Blood (1,200 words; 40-word abstract; 2 figures/tables; 25 references; no fees)
- Blood Work (one peripheral-smear image + 200-word teaching case; ≤ 2 authors)
- Review Article, How I Treat, Blood Spotlight, Perspective, Special Report (invited or by presubmission inquiry; ≤ 3 authors except Special Report)
- Blood Commentary (strictly by invitation)
- Article Comment (1,500 characters; 5 references)
- Case reports: only if they elucidate very novel disease biology or therapy

## Special Notes
Follows the ICMJE Recommendations; over-limit submissions are returned without review, revisions are due within 3 months, and methods cannot be moved to the supplement. Requires a Data Sharing Statement on the title page (individual-participant-data intent for clinical trials), high-throughput data deposited with an accession number at submission, ARRIVE reporting for animal studies, CONSORT for phase 3 RCTs, and strict image-integrity rules; a $105 fee applies per data supplement.
