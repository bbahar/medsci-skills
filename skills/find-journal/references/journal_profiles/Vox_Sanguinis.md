## Scope
Official journal of the International Society of Blood Transfusion, reporting novel developments in transfusion medicine: original papers, reviews, short reports and international fora on all aspects of blood transfusion and cellular therapies, organised in eight main sections.

## Scope Keywords
transfusion medicine, blood transfusion, blood components, blood donors, donor health, transfusion safety, haemovigilance, hemovigilance, immunohaematology, red cell alloimmunization, blood group genotyping, platelet transfusion, plasma, patient blood management, transfusion-transmitted infection, pathogen reduction, cellular therapy, apheresis, massive transfusion, ISBT

## Article Types Accepted
- Original Article (5,000 words total, including references and tables; 250-word structured abstract)
- Review (6,600 words total)
- Short Report (2,500 words total; 200-word abstract; ≤3 figures/tables; ≤12 references)
- Commentary (1,500 words including references; 1 figure + 1 table; contact the Editor-in-Chief first)
- Letter to the Editor (800 words; ≤10 references)
- Report of ISBT Working Party meetings (~6,600 words; contact the Editor-in-Chief first)
- International Forum (topic approved by the section editor)
- Obituary (600 words; at the Editor's discretion)
- Case Reports: not encouraged

## Special Notes
Official ISBT journal with an international remit; single-blind review, and the Editor-in-Chief may return papers judged not competitive or outside readers' interests without review. Word limits are totals that include references, tables and legends; a mandatory cover letter, three bullet-point Highlights, author contributions by initials in Acknowledgements, and a title-page word count are required, and wrongly formatted references can get a paper returned. AI policy: AI tools cannot be authors; any AIGC use must be described in detail in Methods or Acknowledgements (spelling/grammar tools exempt).
